Purpose of this file. This thesis is a conceptual replication of Kaltenecker et al.'s study on the interplay between sampling strategies and machine learning techniques. Using the samppling strategies and subject systems from Kaltenecker et al.'s work on distance-based-sampling paired with the machine learning techniques from Kaltenecker et al.'s work on the aforementioned interplay. The solver-based, distance-based and diversified-distanec-based sampling strategies were NOT written from scratch for this thesis, but rather, they were reused from existing code located in this repository VARA-FEATURE. Because thesee strategies constitute one half of the independent variables in this work's fist research question, their origins are documented here as well as in the "Threats to Validity" section of my work.
